## Prologue

The land of Icewind Dale, for 2 years have been in permafrost near endless night (dim twilight from 10am-2pm). Animals still roam, elf, large fish in unfrozen portion of lake – big enough to bring a man down, but fished still. Supplies are low, help is not near. The people of Ten Towns are left to their own to survive.

North of spine of the World is a portion of the world rarely traveled, except those looking to be beyond civilization…escaping their past. Ten Towns nestled in the ice, not for faint of heart, only those sturdy enough to survive. Winds fall from the east, off the Reghed Glacier, whipping through the land, making its way through every building, armor, etc. sapping strength and energy. Sun no longer holds sway over this area. Icewind Dale falters in the twilight, with long nights of dark and cold. Storm brings sleet and ice, blocking trails leading to warmer lands. There is northern lights – some think it the cause of the cold. People follow the nomadic reindeer, others go into the treacherous waters – finding any source of food they can.
