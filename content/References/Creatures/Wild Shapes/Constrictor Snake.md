### Constrictor Snake

*Medium beast, unaligned*

**Armor Class:** 13
**Temporary Hit Points:** 8
**Speed:** 30 ft., **Swim:** 30 ft.


```ability
abilities:

  wisdom: 20
  charisma: 11
```

**Skills:** Perception +2, Stealth +4
**Senses:** Blindsight 10 ft., Passive Perception 12
**Languages:** Common, Druidic, Elvish
**CR:** 1/4 

## Actions
**Bite:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ `dice: 1d20+4` to hit, reach 5 ft., _Hit:_  `dice:1d8 + 2`piercing damage.
