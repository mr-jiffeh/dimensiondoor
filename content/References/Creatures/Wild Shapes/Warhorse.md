## Actions

**Hooves:**. _Melee Attack Roll:_ `dice: 1d20+6` , reach 10 ft. _Hit:_ `dice: 2d4+4`Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature and the horse moved 20+ feet straight toward it immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 5 `dice: 2d4` Bludgeoning damage and has the Prone condition.
