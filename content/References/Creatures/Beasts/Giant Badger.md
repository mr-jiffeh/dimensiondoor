# Giant Badger

*medium beast, unaligned*

**Armor Class** 10  
**Hit Points** 13 (2d8 + 4)  
**Speed** 30 ft., burrow 10 ft.

|Ability|Score|Bonus|
|---|---|---|
|Strength|13|+1|
|Dexterity|10|+0|
|Constitution|15|+2|
|Intelligence|2|-4|
|Wisdom|12|+1|
|Charisma|5|-3|

**Senses** Darkvision 30 ft., Passive Perception 11  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 1/4 (50 XP)

_**Keen Smell.**_ The badger has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.

### Actions

_**Multiattack.**_ The badger makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws.

_**Bite.**_ Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 4 (1d6 + 1) piercing damage.

_**Claws.**_ Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (2d4 + 1) slashing damage.
