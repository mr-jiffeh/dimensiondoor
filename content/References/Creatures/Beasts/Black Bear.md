# Black Bear

*Medium beast, unaligned*

**Armor Class** 11 (Natural Armor)  
**Hit Points** 19 (3d8 + 6)  
**Speed** 40 ft., climb 30 ft.

|Ability|Score|Bonus|
|---|---|---|
|Strength|15|+2|
|Dexterity|10|+0|
|Constitution|14|+2|
|Intelligence|2|-4|
|Wisdom|12|+1|
|Charisma|7|-2|

**Skills** Perception +3  
**Senses** Passive Perception 13  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 1/2 (100 XP)

_**Keen Smell.**_ The bear has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.

### Actions

_**Multiattack.**_ The bear makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws.

_**Bite.**_ Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.

_**Claws.**_ Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (2d4 + 2) slashing damage.
